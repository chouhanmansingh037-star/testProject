## Review timelines
Every submission must receive a first review within 3 working days. Any item that has been pending for more than 7 calendar days is overdue and must be escalated to the Site Operations Lead. Reviewers work the queue in priority order, not in submission order.

## Priority rules
Safety-critical content (safety equipment, sensor specifications, emergency procedures) is always reviewed first. Folders that other content depends on, such as onboarding material, come second. Videos and images that are purely illustrative come last unless they have been pending longer than 7 calendar days.

## Approving an item
Before approving, open the item and confirm the title matches the content, the file opens and is complete, the folder path is correct, and no personal data or site credentials are visible. Approve only when every check passes. Approval publishes the item immediately to all operators who have access to the folder.

## Rejecting or returning an item
If a check fails, reject the item and write a reason of at least one sentence that tells the submitter what to fix. A rejected item returns to the submitter and leaves the queue. Never approve an item "with comments"; either it meets the rules or it goes back.

## Safety-critical content and escalation
Safety-critical items require a second reviewer sign-off before publication. A reviewer must not approve their own submission. If a reviewer is unsure whether content is accurate, they escalate to the Site Operations Lead instead of guessing. All approvals and rejections are written to the audit log and cannot be edited afterwards.
